How long does it take to build a Squarespace website?

Most projects take between 1–3 weeks depending on scope, number of pages, and how quickly content/feedback is provided.

Yes, you'll need an active Squarespace plan. I can guide you on which plan best fits your needs before we start.

Will my website be mobile responsive?

Absolutely. Every site I build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Can you help with content and copywriting?

Yes, I can assist with structuring and refining your website copy to align with your brand and goals.

Do you offer support after the site is launched?

Yes, I provide post-launch support and training so you can confidently manage your site going forward.
